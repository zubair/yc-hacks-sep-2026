# Postcard visual system

Postcard uses warm cream paper (`#F9F4E9`), deep green ink (`#1B4134`), and a vermilion action accent (`#B83729`). The card is the primary object in compose and conversation screens; forms and navigation remain familiar iOS controls. Photo content has priority on the front. The back uses a divided note/address composition at a readable size, with scrolling for long notes.

Use the system serif for expressive postcard copy and headlines, and the system sans for controls, form labels, and status. Controls use a consistent 12-point corner radius and a 44-point minimum hit area. Paper edges use a restrained border, while the card itself uses a soft downward shadow. All states keep dark text on light paper or light text on dark ink. The demo banner is vermilion and explicitly labels simulated sends.

The principal task is writing and sending a personal message, so visual detail should never hide the recipient choice, the note field, the seal state, or the Send control. Forms scroll above the keyboard, including large type. VoiceOver reads the visible card face only.
