# Motion

Opening turns the photographic front around the vertical axis over 0.8 seconds with ease-in-out timing and reveals the message back. Sealing returns to the front and adds a vermilion seal with a short spring. Sending is a separate explicit button action; neither animation completion nor posture events call the service.

When Reduce Motion is enabled, the face change is a 0.2-second crossfade and the seal fades into place. The hidden face does not receive touch or VoiceOver focus. The card state remains readable if animation is interrupted.
